The top-center crossed swords confirm readiness and start wave 1. Wave 1 has a fully red clock face; every later wave retains a green upper-right quarter. The hand waits at 3 o'clock until the wave's final enemy actually enters, including reinforcements and enemies delayed by a blocked entrance. It then advances clockwise to 12 over 30 seconds of simulation time. At the end of that countdown, a full-map arrow rain deals 200 damage to every active enemy once. Between waves, the hand crosses the fixed green quarter over 15 seconds of simulation time and automatically starts the next wave. There is no numeric countdown badge. Pause and speed affect both timers. Pad captions and entrance/exit captions are omitted.
