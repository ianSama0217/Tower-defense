The HUD uses the user's heart and icon references, edited into transparent sprite atlases with the built-in image generation tool. Original generated files are preserved in the Codex generated_images directory.

- `../../dist/assets/ui/heart-atlas.png`: 4 × 4 equal cells. Frames 0–7 are idle hearts; frames 8–15 are the break animation, ending with an empty frame. Playback is 90 ms per break frame.
- `../../dist/assets/ui/hud-icons.png`: 2 × 2 equal cells: coin, goblin, gear, empty.
- `../../dist/assets/ui/wood-texture.png`: opaque pixel wood material, based on the user's panel reference; UI borders are CSS.
- `../../dist/assets/ui/iron-chain.svg`: code-native, crisp 12 × 36 pixel iron links with highlights and a mounting rivet. Two decorative chains suspend every upper HUD panel; they do not intercept clicks.

HUD plaques use subtle 6 px rounded corners. The swords/clock control shares the HUD wood material, raised edges and paired short iron chains. The drawer has rounded upper corners.

Engineering cards now share one riveted, rounded wooden frame (104 × 140 px on desktop). Artwork, coin amount and hourglass duration are inside the same button, with no separate price frame. Costs are red; demolition proceeds are green. The drawer is 158 px high on desktop and 146 px on narrow screens; four cards fit side by side. New construction and upgrades take 5 game seconds; repairs and demolition take 3. New towers have a build action, show the existing scaffolding/progress animation, and cannot shoot or begin another operation until construction finishes. Destruction cancels construction without refund.

The reference viewport is 1280 × 768. The wooden drawer overlays the lower edge only after selecting a pad. The old handbook, drawer description and close button have been removed; click empty terrain or press Escape to close it. Build, upgrade, repair and demolition prices and durations come from the game configuration.

The top-center crossed swords confirm readiness and start wave 1. Wave 1 has a fully red clock face; every later wave retains a green upper-right quarter. Combat starts at 3 o'clock and advances clockwise to 12 according to kills in that wave, with the combat arc divided by its enemy count. Six enemies therefore advance the hand by 45 degrees per kill; elapsed time and escaped enemies do not advance it. Between waves, the hand crosses the fixed green quarter over 15 seconds of simulation time and automatically starts the next wave. There is no numeric countdown badge. Pause and speed affect the intermission; combat progress follows kills. Pad captions and entrance/exit captions are omitted.
